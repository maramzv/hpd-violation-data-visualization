Why this matters

This was a tested hypothesis, not an assumption: the original prediction was that most buildings resolve their one violation and stay clean, with only a small minority becoming repeat offenders. The data shows the opposite — chronic, recurring violations are the typical outcome for a building that appears in the HPD violations record at all, looking at each building's complete violation history rather than any single time window.
